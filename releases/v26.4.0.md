# v26.4.0
```diff
+ Automatic updates:
- Aura now updates itself: new versions download in the background and install with a restart
- updates are signed, and Aura refuses any download that doesn't match the signature
- while you're connected Aura waits for you to press Update instead of dropping your tunnel
+ Split tunneling:
- pick sites, IPs and ranges that skip Aura and use your normal connection (Settings → Split tunneling)
- one switch to keep your local network (printers, NAS, other subnets) off the tunnel
- needs the new helper; the app prompts you to update it
+ Startup:
- Open at login: Aura starts quietly in the menu bar / tray when you log in
- Connect when Aura opens: reconnects to your last server automatically
```
